Lessons from PPW

Reflections on key topics from the IT4070 Preparation for Professional World module.

// reflection_01
Business Writing & Communication
The Power of Clear, Concise Professional Writing
PPW introduced me to the foundations of business writing — emails, memos, and formal letters. I learned that professional writing must be direct, polite, and concise. Writing instructions like "avoid passive voice," "use formal abbreviations correctly," and "always proofread before sending" seem simple, but applying them consistently changed how I communicate in real workplaces. During my SLT Mobitel internship, these skills helped me write clearer status reports and professional emails to supervisors — something I hadn't expected to matter so much in a software engineering role.
// reflection_02
Interview Skills & STAR Method
Mastering the Art of the Internship Interview
The interview preparation sessions were among the most practically valuable in PPW. Learning about different interview types — behavioral, technical, panel, and virtual — helped demystify a process I found intimidating. The STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) gave me a structured way to answer behavioral questions clearly. Non-verbal communication surprised me most: 65% of meaning in interactions comes from body language, not words. Eye contact, posture, and voice modulation became things I actively practised. These sessions directly prepared me for my SLT Mobitel interview, where I applied these techniques and succeeded.
// reflection_03
Presentation Skills
Delivering Ideas with Confidence and Structure
The presentation skills module taught me that a strong presentation is structured — a brief introduction to capture attention, a main body with clear signposting as you move between points, and a concise conclusion that leaves a lasting impression. I learned that effective delivery depends on five key factors: eye contact, voice modulation, expression, appearance, and posture. Applying these in my SnoutScan research presentations made a real difference — I received feedback that my explanations were clear and my technical content was well-structured and accessible to a non-technical audience.
// reflection_04
Career Planning & Professional Identity
Building a Career with Intention, Not Accident
PPW challenged me to think intentionally about my career rather than letting it happen by default. Crafting my CV taught me to communicate my technical skills and project experience concisely — a critical skill for standing out to recruiters. Understanding what employers assess beyond skills — communication ability, cultural fit, and professionalism — shifted how I approach every interaction. The module reinforced that technical ability is the entry ticket, but how you carry yourself, communicate, and grow defines your long-term career. I now approach every project and internship as a deliberate step toward becoming the engineer I want to be.

// problem_statement

Millions of dogs go missing each year. Traditional methods (tags, microchips) are error-prone or require specialist scanners. No single system currently combines biometric verification, multi-view image recognition, adaptive geolocation, and privacy-aware communication into one cohesive workflow.

My component addresses this with a hybrid progressive identification pipeline — broad image matching narrowing to high-confidence biometric verification, paired with community alerting and encrypted coordination.

// model_architecture · DualViewFusionModel

🧠 Frontal Encoder
EfficientNet-B0 (ImageNet pretrained). Captures local textures — facial markings, coat colouring.
👁️ Lateral Encoder
Vision Transformer ViT-B/16. Captures global spatial structure — body silhouette.
🔀 Fusion Module
Projection heads → L2 norm → concatenation → FC layers → 512-dim embedding.
📉 Training
Hard Triplet Loss + ArcFace. AdamW + cosine annealing LR schedule. 50 epochs.

// system_modules

  • Adaptive Geolocation Alerts: Tier-based radius expansion (500m→2km→5km→10km→20km) using Haversine formula. Background job evaluates elapsed time per report, dispatches FCM push notifications. Deduplication log prevents repeat alerts per tier/user.
  • Secure In-App Chat: Firebase Firestore + custom auth tokens. Access-controlled conversations between finder, owner, and shelters. Real-time snapshot listeners.
  • Fallback Strategy: Unmatched found dogs are auto-forwarded to nearest registered shelter with images and candidate matches.
  • Flutter Frontend: Dog submission screen, watch-area management, chat UI, push notification routing via Provider pattern.

// abstract

The rising number of missing dogs highlights the urgent need for an innovative, non-invasive, and privacy-focused recovery solution. This system integrates dual-view image matching, biometric nose print verification, and adaptive geolocation alerts to accurately identify lost dogs and facilitate rapid reunification with owners.

The deep learning model combines CNNs and Vision Transformers to analyse frontal and lateral dog images. A biometric verification module compares unique nose print patterns. The adaptive geolocation alert mechanism dynamically adjusts search radius. An encrypted in-app communication channel ensures privacy-aware coordination.
